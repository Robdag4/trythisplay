Cover 3 is one of the most common defenses you will see in Madden 27 ranked play
and franchise games alike. It protects deep with three defenders while keeping
four underneath zones, which makes it strong against vertical shots but
vulnerable in the seams and flats.

## Identify it before the snap

Start every play with the same checklist. One safety deep in the middle of the
field is your first clue. Corners aligned seven or more yards off the ball, or
visibly bailing at the snap, confirm zone shells. Send a receiver in motion: if
no defender follows across the formation, you are looking at zone, and a single
high safety makes Cover 3 the most likely call.

## Attack the seams

The space between the deep outside thirds and the deep middle third is the
structural weakness of Cover 3. Vertical routes from slot receivers or tight
ends split that space. Throw with touch over the hook defenders and in front of
the free safety, and lead the receiver toward the open grass.

## Flood one side

Three-level flood concepts put a deep route, a corner route, and a flat route
on the same sideline. The flat defender cannot cover both the flat and the
corner route behind him. Read flat defender first: if he sinks under the
corner route, throw the flat; if he widens with the flat, hit the corner route
over his head.

## Work the middle with drags

Underneath crossing routes stress the hook zones. Pair a drag with a deeper
in-breaking route behind it so the hook defenders have to choose. Against
heavier pass rushes, the drag also doubles as your hot answer.

## Common mistakes

Do not force outside vertical routes into the deep thirds, and do not hold the
ball waiting for deep posts to clear the free safety. Cover 3 wants you to be
impatient. Take the seams, the flats, and the corners until the defense
adjusts, then take your shot.
