Gun Bunch compresses three receivers to one side of the formation, creating
stacked releases that are difficult to press and natural traffic that rubs
defenders off in man coverage. In Madden 27 it remains one of the most
scheme-rich formations in the game: flood concepts attack Cover 3, mesh and
spot concepts beat man, and the isolated backside receiver gives you a
one-on-one shot when defenses overload the bunch.

Protect the bunch-side edge against zone blitzes, and be ready to check into
quicker concepts when defenses show pressure off that side.
