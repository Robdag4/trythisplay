Simulation Franchise

The Simulation Franchise is a realistic, NFL-style online league. You own a team for the long haul: manage the salary cap, scout and draft, develop players, negotiate trades that pass a realism review, and compete through weekly advances with power rankings and league stories covering every storyline.

League rules

  1. 01 Play calling must follow simulation guidelines: realistic run-pass balance, situational football, and no exploit schemes.
  2. 02 All trades are submitted for commissioner review and must pass a realism check before processing.
  3. 03 Salary cap rules are enforced; teams must remain cap compliant through the league year.
  4. 04 Weekly advances run on the published schedule; unplayed games may be simulated.
  5. 05 Team owners are expected to stay active in Discord and complete games on time.

Frequently asked questions

Do I need to be an elite player to join? +

No. The Simulation Franchise values realistic play and activity over pure stick skill. Moderate to advanced players who love team building fit best.

How are trades approved? +

Trades are submitted in Discord and reviewed for realism by the commissioner team before being processed in the league.

What happens if I miss an advance? +

Games left unplayed at the end of an advance window may be simulated. Repeated inactivity can lead to your team being opened to a new owner.
