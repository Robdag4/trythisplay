Competitive Franchise

The Competitive Franchise is built for players who want serious head-to-head Madden 27. Every game is a scheduled user matchup played under an advanced competitive rule set, with rankings, standings, tournaments, and a seasonal champion crowned at the end of each cycle.

Competition

  • Scheduled user games every advance
  • Season standings and rankings
  • Playoff race and seasonal champion
  • Tournaments between seasons

Community

  • Dedicated Competitive Discord server
  • Matchmaking and scrimmage channels
  • Strategy discussion with other members
  • League announcements and results

Structure

  • Commissioner-managed advances
  • Advanced competitive rule set
  • Entry review for skill fit
  • Team selection from available franchises

League rules

  1. 01 All league games are user versus user and must be played within the scheduled advance window.
  2. 02 The competitive rule set governs play calling, no banned exploits, glitches, or unrealistic repeat-play abuse.
  3. 03 Quitting or dashboarding a league game without commissioner approval results in a forfeit.
  4. 04 Disputes are reported in the Discord dispute channel with video evidence where possible.
  5. 05 Repeated rule violations lead to removal from the league.

Apply to join

Applications are reviewed by the commissioners. Approved members receive a Discord invitation and team assignment. Submit your application through the contact form and select the franchise application inquiry type, or apply directly in Discord once connected.

Frequently asked questions

What skill level do I need for the Competitive Franchise? +

The league is aimed at advanced players comfortable with user games, hot routes, and pre-snap adjustments. If you are newer, start with our beginner ebooks and guides, then apply when you are winning consistently online.

How often do games advance? +

Advance schedules are set by the commissioners each season and announced in Discord. Games must be completed within the advance window or scheduled with your opponent in the matchmaking channels.

How do I get my team? +

After your application is approved, you pick from the available teams list in Discord on a first-approved, first-pick basis.
