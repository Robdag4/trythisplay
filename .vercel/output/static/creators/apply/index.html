Creator Program

Turn Your Madden Knowledge Into Income

Try This Play is an invitation-only creator marketplace. Apply below, and if approved you'll get access to a private portal with everything you need to build, submit, and sell premium video ebooks.

  1. 01

    Apply

    Tell us about your Madden experience, achievements, content, and the product you want to build.

  2. 02

    Get reviewed

    Our team reviews every application. Approved creators receive a private invitation to the creator portal, applying does not automatically create an account.

  3. 03

    Build your ebook

    Upload video lessons, review auto-generated transcripts and written setups, add cover art, and preview your product exactly as customers will see it.

  4. 04

    Submit and earn

    Submit for review, respond to any change requests, and earn your revenue share on every sale, paid out through Stripe.

Quality and ownership expectations

  • You created, or have documented permission to use, everything in your product: video, audio, graphics, and written material.
  • Products teach complete, working setups, not recycled clips or thin content.
  • Setups are kept current: creators update products when patches change the meta.
  • Every AI-drafted written setup is reviewed and approved by you before it reaches customers.

Apply now

Send your application through the contact form with the Creator Application inquiry type. Include your gamertag, social profiles, Discord username, Madden experience, competitive achievements, links to content examples, your preferred product category, estimated audience size, and a short product idea.

A dedicated application form with structured fields ships with the creator portal in the next release.
