Gun Bunch

Offense · 1RB 1TE 3WR · Reviewed August 1, 2026

Strengths

  • Stacked releases beat press man
  • Natural rubs and picks
  • Compressed spacing floods zones

Weaknesses

  • Vulnerable to zone blitzes off the bunch side
  • Backside can be isolated

Best situations

  • Second and medium
  • Red zone
  • Against press man coverage

Gun Bunch compresses three receivers to one side of the formation, creating stacked releases that are difficult to press and natural traffic that rubs defenders off in man coverage. In Madden 27 it remains one of the most scheme-rich formations in the game: flood concepts attack Cover 3, mesh and spot concepts beat man, and the isolated backside receiver gives you a one-on-one shot when defenses overload the bunch.

Protect the bunch-side edge against zone blitzes, and be ready to check into quicker concepts when defenses show pressure off that side.
