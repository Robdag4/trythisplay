Common questions

What is a Madden video ebook? +

A video ebook is a private digital product that combines video lessons with structured written setups: formations, audibles, pre-snap adjustments, reads, coverage beaters, blitz counters, and full transcripts. You get instant access after purchase in your library.

Do ebooks get updated during the Madden season? +

Yes. When patches change the meta, creators can revise setups and add lessons. Your library shows the current Madden version, the last-updated date, and update notes for every product you own.

Which franchise should I join: Competitive or Simulation? +

Join the Competitive Franchise if you care about rankings, tournaments, and high-level user games. Join the Simulation Franchise if you want realistic NFL-style team building with trades, salary cap management, drafts, and weekly league content.

How do I become a Try This Play creator? +

Apply through the creator application page. Our team reviews applications, and approved creators receive a private invitation to the creator portal where they can upload lessons, review generated written setups, and submit products for review.
