# Ebook Cover Art Specification

Specs for product cover images on TryThisPlay.com. Give this document to any
designer (or AI image tool) producing cover art.

## Dimensions

| Property | Value |
|---|---|
| Aspect ratio | **4:5 portrait (exact)** |
| Recommended size | **1600 × 2000 px** |
| Minimum size | 1200 × 1500 px |
| Format | JPG (photographic) or PNG (flat graphics) |
| Max file size | 1 MB target (compress before upload) |
| Color space | sRGB |

Images that are not exactly 4:5 are center-cropped automatically — the long
dimension gets trimmed. Always deliver at exactly 4:5.

## Safe zones

The site renders covers with a 3D book effect (spine on the left, page curl
on the right) and also as small grid thumbnails (~300 px tall).

- Keep all text, logos, and critical elements inside the **center 80%**
  of the canvas — nothing within 8% of the left or right edge.
- Backgrounds may bleed to all edges.
- Title must remain legible at thumbnail size: test at 240 × 300 px.
- Design for dark surroundings: the page background is near-black
  (#0A0A0B). Covers with a dark outer edge blend in; use contrast or the
  brand red (#E11D2E) to pop.

## Brand alignment (recommended, not enforced)

- Brand palette: red #E11D2E, black #0A0A0B, charcoal #121214,
  silver #9CA1A8, white #F7F7F8.
- Display typography on the site is Anton (condensed uppercase). Covers
  don't have to use it, but condensed bold uppercase titles match the
  storefront best.
- The site's signature motif is play-diagram route lines (X's, O's, route
  arrows) — a strong, ownable visual language for covers.

## Content rules

- **No third-party trademarks or logos** (NFL team logos, EA/Madden marks,
  player likenesses, broadcast imagery). Team *names* in plain text as a
  factual description (e.g. "Arizona Offense") are a different category
  than logos, but when in doubt, use original artwork only.
- No screenshots of the game itself on covers.
- No fake endorsements, review quotes, or badges.
- Original artwork or properly licensed assets only — creators confirm
  ownership at submission.
